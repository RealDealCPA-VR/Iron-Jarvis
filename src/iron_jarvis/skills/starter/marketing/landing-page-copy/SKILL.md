---
name: landing-page-copy
description: >-
  Write the words for one web page that should get a visitor to take one action.
---

Use this when the user needs copy for a landing page, a service page or a sign-up page.

1. Fix the one action the page wants (book a call, buy, sign up). Everything on the page serves it.
2. Write, in order:
   - **Headline**: the result the customer gets, in their words.
   - **Subheadline**: who it is for and what it is.
   - **Three to five benefits**: each a short heading and one sentence.
   - **How it works**: three steps.
   - **Proof**: only real reviews, numbers or names the user gave you. If none, leave a marked gap.
   - **Questions and answers**: the three objections a buyer is most likely to have.
   - **Call to action**: the same words on every button.
3. Write at a level a twelve-year-old could follow. Short sentences.
4. Give two alternative headlines.
5. Never write a guarantee, a price or a claim the user did not give you.
