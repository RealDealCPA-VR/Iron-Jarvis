---
name: campaign-brief
description: >-
  Write a one-page campaign brief: goal, audience, message, channels, budget and how success is measured.
---

Use this when the user is planning a promotion, a launch or any marketing push.

1. Ask for anything missing that you cannot work without: what is being promoted, to whom, and by when.
2. Write the brief with these parts:
   - **Goal**: one measurable result and the date.
   - **Audience**: who, and what they care about.
   - **Message**: the one thing they should remember, and the proof for it.
   - **Offer and call to action**.
   - **Channels**: where it runs and why each one.
   - **Timeline**: key dates.
   - **Budget**: only figures the user gave.
   - **Measure**: the numbers that will show whether it worked.
3. Keep it to one page.
4. Do not invent statistics, customer quotes or results. Mark anything to be confirmed as "to confirm".
