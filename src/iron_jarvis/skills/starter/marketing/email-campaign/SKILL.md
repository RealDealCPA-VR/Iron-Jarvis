---
name: email-campaign
description: >-
  Write a marketing email or a short series: subject lines, body and one clear call to action.
---

Use this when the user wants to email customers or a list.

1. Confirm who is receiving it and what one thing they should do.
2. For each email give:
   - three subject lines under 50 characters,
   - a preview line,
   - the body: a first sentence about the reader, the point, the offer, and one call to action,
   - a plain-text version.
3. For a series, say what each email is for and how many days apart they go.
4. One call to action per email. Repeat it once.
5. Include the unsubscribe line and the sender's postal address as placeholders; the law in many places requires both.
6. No invented discounts, deadlines or scarcity. Use only what the user gave.
7. Do not send. Give the drafts for review.
