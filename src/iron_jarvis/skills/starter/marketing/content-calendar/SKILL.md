name: content-calendar
description: >-
  Plan a month of content: dates, topics, formats and who does what.
---

Use this when the user wants a plan for posts, articles, emails or videos over a period.

1. Ask how often they can really publish and on which channels, if not given. A plan they cannot keep is worse than a small one.
2. Choose three to five themes that fit the business and its customers.
3. Build a table: Date, Channel, Theme, Topic, Format, Call to action, Owner, Status.
4. Spread the themes evenly. Put time-sensitive items (holidays, deadlines, events) in first.
5. For each topic give a working title and one sentence on the angle.
6. Reuse on purpose: note where one piece can become several.
7. Save it as a spreadsheet if the user asks for a file, with one row per item.
8. Do not schedule or publish anything. This is a plan.
