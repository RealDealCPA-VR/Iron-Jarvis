---
name: bank-reconciliation-review
description: >-
  Review a bank or credit card reconciliation and list what does not tie or looks wrong.
---

Use this when the user gives a reconciliation report, a statement and a ledger, or asks why an account does not tie.

1. State the three numbers first: statement ending balance, book balance, and the difference.
2. List every reconciling item: outstanding cheques, deposits in transit, bank fees, interest, errors.
3. Flag for attention:
   - items outstanding more than 60 days,
   - duplicate amounts on the same or next day,
   - round amounts with no description,
   - transactions dated after the statement date,
   - anything changed after a prior reconciliation.
4. Work out every total with a calculation tool or a spreadsheet formula. Do not add figures in your head, and re-check each total before you write it.
5. If the pieces do not explain the whole difference, say how much is unexplained. Never force it to tie.
6. End with the entries needed to correct the books, as debit and credit lines, for the user to approve.
