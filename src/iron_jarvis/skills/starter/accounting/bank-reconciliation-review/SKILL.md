---
name: bank-reconciliation-review
description: >-
  Review a bank or credit card reconciliation and list what does not tie or looks wrong.
---

Use this when the user gives a reconciliation report, a statement and a ledger, or asks why an account does not tie.

1. State the three numbers first: statement ending balance, book balance, and the difference.
2. If the statement and the ledger are both spreadsheets, match them with the transaction matching tool (excel_reconcile) when you have it. Do not match rows by reading them. Report what it says: how many pairs matched, every row left over on each side, and whether the left-over rows explain the whole difference. If it says the two lists use opposite signs, run it again with one side turned.
3. List every reconciling item: outstanding cheques, deposits in transit, bank fees, interest, errors. A row the tool lists as a possible group (one deposit made of several receipts) is yours to judge: say so, do not count it as matched.
4. Flag for attention:
   - items outstanding more than 60 days,
   - duplicate amounts on the same or next day,
   - round amounts with no description,
   - transactions dated after the statement date,
   - anything changed after a prior reconciliation.
5. Work out every total with a calculation tool or a spreadsheet formula. Do not add figures in your head, and re-check each total before you write it.
6. If the pieces do not explain the whole difference, say how much is unexplained. Never force it to tie.
7. End with the entries needed to correct the books, as debit and credit lines, for the user to approve.
