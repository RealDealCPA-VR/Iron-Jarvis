---
name: month-end-close-checklist
description: >-
  Build or run a month-end close checklist for a small business's books.
---

Use this when the user is closing a month or wants a close checklist for a client.

1. Ask which accounting system and which month, if not given.
2. Go through the close in this order and report each as done, not done or not applicable:
   - bank and credit card accounts reconciled to statements,
   - undeposited funds and uncleared items reviewed,
   - accounts receivable aged and old balances questioned,
   - accounts payable aged and unrecorded bills checked,
   - payroll and payroll taxes recorded,
   - loans, prepaid items, fixed assets and depreciation entries made,
   - sales tax and other liabilities tied to filings,
   - suspense, uncategorised and "ask my accountant" accounts cleared,
   - profit and loss compared with last month and the same month last year.
3. For each item not done, say exactly what is missing and who must supply it.
4. Never mark an item done from memory: only from a report, a statement or the user's word.
5. End with a short list for the client: the documents and answers still needed.
