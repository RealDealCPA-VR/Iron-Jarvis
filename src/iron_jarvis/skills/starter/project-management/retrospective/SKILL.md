---
name: retrospective
description: >-
  Run a short review after a project or milestone: what worked, what did not, what to change.
---

Use this at the end of a project, a release or a difficult week.

1. Collect the facts first: what was planned, what happened, and the dates. Use the record, not memory, where there is one.
2. Sort what the user or team said into:
   - **Keep doing**,
   - **Stop doing**,
   - **Start doing**,
   - **Still puzzling**.
3. For each problem ask why until you reach something that can be changed. Look at the way of working, not at who is to blame.
4. Choose at most three changes. Each gets an owner and a date, and a way to tell whether it helped.
5. Write it up in one page.
6. Start the next review by checking the three changes from this one.
