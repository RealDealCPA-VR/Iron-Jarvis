---
name: risk-register
description: >-
  List what could go wrong in a project, how likely and how bad, and what to do about each.
---

Use this when planning a project or when a project feels shaky.

1. List risks from what you know about this project, not a generic list. Cover: people, time, money, scope, suppliers, technology, and rules that apply.
2. For each risk give: what could happen, what would cause it, likelihood (low, medium, high), impact (low, medium, high), the early warning sign, what to do now to make it less likely, what to do if it happens, and an owner.
3. Sort by likelihood and impact together, worst first.
4. Keep it to the risks worth watching. Ten real ones beat forty vague ones.
5. Say which ratings are your judgement.
6. Give it as a table, and name the three risks to raise with whoever decides.
