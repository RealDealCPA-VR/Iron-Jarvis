---
name: project-kickoff-brief
description: >-
  Write a kickoff brief: goal, scope, people, dates, and what is not included.
---

Use this at the start of a project or when a project has no written plan.

1. Ask for what you cannot work without: the goal, the deadline, and who decides.
2. Write one page:
   - **Goal**: the result, and how everyone will know it is reached.
   - **In scope** and **Not in scope**: the second list prevents most arguments later.
   - **People**: who decides, who does the work, who must be told.
   - **Milestones**: dates for the main steps.
   - **Budget and limits**: only what the user gave.
   - **Risks**: the three biggest.
   - **How we work**: where updates go and how often.
3. Mark every assumption as an assumption.
4. End with the decisions needed before work starts.
