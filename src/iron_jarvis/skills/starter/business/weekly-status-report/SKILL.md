---
name: weekly-status-report
description: >-
  Write a short weekly status report: done, in progress, blocked, and next week.
---

Use this when the user wants to report progress to a manager, a client or a team.

1. Gather what happened from what the user gives you. Do not list work nobody mentioned.
2. Write four short sections:
   - **Done this week**: finished items, most important first.
   - **In progress**: each with how far along and the expected date.
   - **Blocked or at risk**: what is stuck, why, and who can unblock it.
   - **Next week**: the three to five things that matter most.
3. Lead with a one-sentence summary: on track, at risk, or behind, and why.
4. Use numbers where the user gave them. Never estimate a percentage on your own.
5. Keep the same headings every week so reports can be compared.
