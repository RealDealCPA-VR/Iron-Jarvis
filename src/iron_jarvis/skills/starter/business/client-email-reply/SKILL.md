---
name: client-email-reply
description: >-
  Draft a clear, polite reply to a client email, ready to copy and send.
---

Use this when the user pastes a client's email or describes one and wants a reply.

1. Work out what the client is asking for: list each question or request to yourself.
2. Answer every one. If you cannot answer one from what you have, ask the user for that fact before drafting; do not fill it in.
3. Write the reply as a draft the user will send:
   - a subject line,
   - a first sentence that answers the main question,
   - short paragraphs, one topic each,
   - a clear next step and who takes it,
   - the user's usual sign-off if you know it.
4. Plain words. No legal or technical terms the client did not use first, unless you explain them.
5. Never promise a date, a price or an outcome the user did not give you.
6. Do not send anything. Give the draft and say what you assumed.
