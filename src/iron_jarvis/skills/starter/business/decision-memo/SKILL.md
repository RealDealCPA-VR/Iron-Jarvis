---
name: decision-memo
description: >-
  Lay out a decision on one page: the question, the options, what each costs and risks, and a recommendation.
---

Use this when the user has to choose between options or explain a choice to someone else.

1. State the decision as one question.
2. Give the background in three sentences or fewer: only what the reader needs.
3. List the options, including "do nothing". For each: what it is, cost, time, risk, and what it depends on. Use a table when there are three or more.
4. Say which facts are known and which are your estimate. Label every estimate.
5. Give one recommendation and the reason in two sentences.
6. Say what would change the recommendation.
7. End with what you need from the reader and by when.
8. Keep it to one page. Any figure you write must come from the user's material or be labelled an estimate.
