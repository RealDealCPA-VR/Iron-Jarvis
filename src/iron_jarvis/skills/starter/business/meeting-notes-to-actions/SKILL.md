---
name: meeting-notes-to-actions
description: >-
  Turn meeting notes or a transcript into decisions, action items with owners and dates, and open questions.
---

Use this when the user gives notes, a transcript or a recording summary from a meeting.

1. Read all of it before writing anything. Do not add anything that was not said.
2. Write three sections, in this order:
   - **Decisions**: what was agreed, one line each.
   - **Action items**: a table with Action, Owner, Due date. Use the person's name as written. If no owner or date was said, write "not assigned" or "no date"; never invent one.
   - **Open questions**: what was raised and not settled.
3. Keep each line short enough to read on a phone.
4. End with one line: who should receive this summary, if the notes say.
5. If the notes are unclear about who agreed to what, say so in Open questions instead of guessing.
