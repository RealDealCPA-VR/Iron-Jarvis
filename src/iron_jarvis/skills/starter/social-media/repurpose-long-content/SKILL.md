---
name: repurpose-long-content
description: >-
  Turn one long piece (an article, a video, a talk) into several short posts.
---

Use this when the user has a long piece of content and wants more use from it.

1. Read or watch the summary of the whole piece. List its five to eight separate points.
2. For each point write one short post that stands on its own: the point, one example from the piece, and a link or pointer back.
3. Add: one post that quotes a strong line, one that asks a question the piece answers, and one list post.
4. Say which platform each suits and what image or clip it needs.
5. Keep the author's meaning. Do not add facts that are not in the piece.
6. Suggest an order and spacing so they do not all go out at once.
7. Do not post. Give the drafts for review.
