---
name: social-performance-review
description: >-
  Review how posts performed from the numbers, and say what to do more or less of.
---

Use this when the user gives post statistics or an export from a platform.

1. Say what period and which platforms the numbers cover, and what is missing.
2. Calculate the figures with a tool or a spreadsheet, not in your head: totals, averages per post, and the rate of engagement where the data allows it.
3. Name the three best and three weakest posts by the measure that matters to the goal (reach, clicks, replies, enquiries), and what they have in common: topic, type, day, first line.
4. Separate what the data shows from what is a guess. A handful of posts cannot prove a pattern; say so.
5. Give three things to do more of, two to stop, and one experiment for next month with how it will be judged.
6. Keep it to one page with one small table.
