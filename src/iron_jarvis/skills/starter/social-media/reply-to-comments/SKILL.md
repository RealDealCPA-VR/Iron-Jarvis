---
name: reply-to-comments
description: >-
  Draft replies to comments, reviews and messages, including unhappy ones.
---

Use this when the user pastes comments, reviews or direct messages and wants replies.

1. Sort them: praise, a question, a complaint, spam or abuse.
2. Praise: thank them by name and mention what they said.
3. Question: answer it in the reply. If you do not know the answer, say what the user needs to tell you.
4. Complaint: acknowledge it, do not argue, say what will happen next, and move the detail to a private channel. Never share or confirm anything about a customer's account in public.
5. Spam or abuse: recommend hiding or reporting; write no reply.
6. Keep each reply short and in the business's usual voice.
7. Flag anything that needs the owner or a lawyer: threats, legal claims, safety, or the press.
8. Do not post. Give the drafts for review.
