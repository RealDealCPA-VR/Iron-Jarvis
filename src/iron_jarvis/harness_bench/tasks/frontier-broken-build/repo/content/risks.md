# What could go wrong

- Supply delays: lead times for the "core" boards are up to 14 weeks.
- A new data rule: exports must not contain <email> addresses.
- Currency: a 5% swing in EUR moves revenue by about $0.4M.

# What we are doing

We moved two suppliers to *quarterly* contracts and added a check to the
`export` job.
