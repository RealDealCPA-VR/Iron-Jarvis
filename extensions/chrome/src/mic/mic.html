Let Iron Jarvis hear you

The sidebar's microphone needs your browser's permission once, and your browser only asks on a page like this one — never inside the sidebar.

  • Press the button. Your browser will ask whether Iron Jarvis may use the microphone.
  • Choose Allow. Nothing is recorded here: the microphone is opened and closed again, only to earn the permission.
  • Go back to the sidebar and press the microphone. What you say goes to Iron Jarvis on this computer, never to a cloud speech service.

If no prompt appears, your browser has already blocked the microphone for this add-on: click the icon left of the address bar on this page, set Microphone to Allow, then press the button again.
