Let Iron Jarvis work in your tabs

The Iron Jarvis browser add-on installs with no access to any site. Your browser only lets a page ask for that access when you click a button yourself, which is what this page is for.

What site access lets Iron Jarvis do

  • Read what is on the pages you open — the text, the links, the forms.
  • Act in a page when you ask: click, type, fill a form, scroll, take a screenshot, open or close a tab.
  • Each action is shown to you first in Iron Jarvis, unless you allowed a tab or switched asking off in the sidebar; a payment, a password field or anything that deletes always asks.
  • Everything runs on this computer, over 127.0.0.1; nothing is sent to a service of ours.
  • You can withdraw this access at any time from your browser's add-ons page, its add-ons page.

You can close this tab once access is granted.
