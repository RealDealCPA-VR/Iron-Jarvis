# What changed in Iron Jarvis

*One paragraph per version, made from the Handbook's own sections. Settings › System › Updates shows the paragraph for the version you are on; the Handbook holds the full story of each change.*

## v1.511.0

**Every OK is for what will really happen.** The third of eight releases from the second review.

## v1.510.0

**Nothing is lost, part two.** The second of eight releases from the second review.

## v1.509.0

**Other computers and web pages stay outside.** A second review read the parts of Iron Jarvis the first one skipped (coding sessions, the browser, your other computers) and found 98 more things to fix.

## v1.508.0

**A coding session recovers from its own mistakes.** Read off a real coding session with a team, where helpers kept repeating failed steps.

## v1.507.1

**It learns from what went wrong.** The last part of the review fixes: Iron Jarvis now notices when something went wrong and asks whether to remember it.

## v1.506.0

**Ask for anything in chat.** The sixth part of the review fixes: things the app could do that chat could not reach, could not find, or could not point you to.

## v1.505.1

**It keeps going, and asks once.** The fifth part of the review fixes: places where work could hang with nobody told, and places where Iron Jarvis asked the same question again and again.

## v1.504.0

**Nothing is lost.** The fourth part of the review fixes: the places where your work, or the record of it, could disappear without a way back.

## v1.503.0

**"Done" means done.** The third part of the review fixes.

## v1.502.0

**Nothing leaves your PC as a side effect.** The second part of the review fixes.

## v1.501.0

**One client's notes stay with that client, and Iron Jarvis asks before it keeps what it guessed.** A review of the app against its own promises found places where something learned during one client's work could be told in another client's chat, and places where Iron Jarvis kept what a model guessed as if you had said it.

## v1.500.2

**Resize the panel beside a coding session.** In Chat › Code and Build › Code, the panel on the right (plan, timeline, changes, usage, files and the rest) can now be made wider or narrower: drag its left edge.

## v1.500.1

**Archive or delete a coding session from the list.** In Chat › Code, every session in the sidebar list now has a ⋯ button (it shows when you point at the row).

## v1.500.0

**One sidebar.** The app now has one left sidebar.

## v1.499.4

**A deep review of the whole app: fixes for privacy, safety and lost work.** Seven reviewers read the whole app area by area; every finding below was reproduced or traced in the code before it was fixed, and each fix has a test that failed first.

## v1.499.2

**The rest of the review: clients, terminal help and pairing.** **Your answer about a client is never undone by Jarvis.** When you pressed Yes or No on a name at the same moment Jarvis was looking through your folders again, that look could delete the name you had just answered about.

## v1.499.1

**Five fixes from a second review of clients, checking and pairing.** **A client's work stays with that client in one more case.** A chat with no folder of its own, say in Downloads, that used files from a client's folder and also from a folder you have not answered about yet could be shown to an unrelated chat.

## v1.499.0

**Iron Jarvis knows your clients.** **What it is.** Iron Jarvis now knows which of your folders belong to which client.

## v1.498.0

**The "keep working until it's true" check, everywhere and in more languages.** v1.495.0 checked chat replies and agent runs, in English.

## v1.497.0

**Pair two computers in one step.** **What it is.** If you run Iron Jarvis on two computers (an office PC and a laptop, or two offices), you can pair them.
