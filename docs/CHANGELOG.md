# What changed in Iron Jarvis

*One paragraph per version, made from the Handbook's own sections. Settings › System › Updates shows the paragraph for the version you are on; the Handbook holds the full story of each change.*

## v1.541.0

**One place for where your work goes, and plainer words.** The last of nine releases from the fourth review.

## v1.540.0

**Linux, the right file always.** The eighth of nine releases from the fourth review.

## v1.539.0

**Terminals stay on your account, and your folder stays yours.** The seventh of nine releases from the fourth review.

## v1.538.0

**Connected apps keep working by themselves.** The sixth of nine releases from the fourth review.

## v1.537.0

**A tidier sidebar: Terminals, Other tools and Projects start folded.** The lower part of the sidebar now starts out of the way, and you open what you want with one press.

## v1.536.0

**A hand-off is never lost.** The fifth of nine releases from the fourth review.

## v1.535.0

**Stop says who, and acts at once.** The fourth of nine releases from the fourth review.

## v1.534.0

**Done means done, for attempts and the night.** The third of nine releases from the fourth review.

## v1.533.0

**Every new address asks, and cards say why.** The second of nine releases from the fourth review.

## v1.532.0

**What other apps are told.** The first of nine releases from the fourth review.

## v1.531.0

**Fold the other tools away.** The **Other tools** part of a list (Claude Code and Codex in Chat › Code, Claude Chat in the chat list) now folds.

## v1.530.0

**Watch Claude Code and Codex from Chat › Code, and they see Iron Jarvis.** Your Claude Code and Codex sessions now sit where coding happens: in **Chat › Code**, beside your Iron Jarvis sessions.

## v1.529.0

**Every model call counted, and the open decisions settled.** Eight of the ten points the third fix plan left for you to decide, built as recommended.

## v1.528.0

**Claude desktop's Chat tab and Codex can record their chats here.** Claude desktop keeps the chats in its **Chat** tab on claude.ai, not on this PC, so Iron Jarvis cannot read them.

## v1.527.0

**Plain words, and nothing dropped without a word.** The last of seven releases from the third review: coding sessions say why they stopped waiting or could not save, failures read as sentences, and the start-up no longer slows down as the history grows.

## v1.526.0

**It keeps working across a restart and a dropped connection.** A coding tool that was running when Iron Jarvis restarted gets Iron Jarvis's tools back by itself, a reply in the browser sidebar follows the sidebar when its connection drops and returns, and a terminal that cannot be reopened is kept and shown instead of vanishing.

## v1.525.0

**Your other tools' chats, tidied after a look in the real window.** The "Other tools" part of the chat list (v1.518.0) was looked at with the chats on a real PC, and three things were wrong.

## v1.524.0

**What the screen says is what happens.** Where a button or a line named one thing and Iron Jarvis did another, the two now agree: "Continue on X" goes to X, a setting changed while a reply runs says when it takes effect, and a box you turned off stays off.

## v1.523.0

**Done means done at night, and a picture is described only by a model that saw it.** A night task tried on several models no longer reads "Done" whatever its attempts did, its cost is counted, and the night's limits apply to every model it is tried on.

## v1.522.0

**Stop means stopped, and it is remembered.** A Stop is now written down the moment you press it, so a reply you stopped is never carried on by Iron Jarvis itself, and a Stop pressed too early or at a waiting card can no longer be lost.
