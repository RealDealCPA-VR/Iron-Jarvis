# What changed in Iron Jarvis

*One paragraph per version, made from the Handbook's own sections. Settings › System › Updates shows the paragraph for the version you are on; the Handbook holds the full story of each change.*

## v1.522.0

**Stop means stopped, and it is remembered.** A Stop is now written down the moment you press it, so a reply you stopped is never carried on by Iron Jarvis itself, and a Stop pressed too early or at a waiting card can no longer be lost.

## v1.521.0

**A reply lives in its chat, whoever was watching.** A reply that finishes while you are on another page, or with the window closed, is now written into its chat by Iron Jarvis itself, and the browser sidebar's conversation is a chat like the others.

## v1.520.0

**Every way in is judged, and what a program prints stays data.** A new tool in a connected app now asks before it runs even when its app is switched on for the chat, and sixteen other places where something reached a tool, a file or a model past the usual check are closed.

## v1.519.0

**Linux: start at sign-in, the tray icon, Edge, and true words.** Nothing changes on Windows in this release.

## v1.518.0

**Your Claude Code and Codex chats, in the chat list and in memory.** Chats you had in Claude Code (the desktop app's Code tab, or a terminal) and in Codex now show up in Iron Jarvis, and the work done in them counts as work done in that folder.

## v1.517.0

**The Linux app updates itself, and comes as two files.** Nothing changes on Windows in this release.

## v1.516.1

**Linux: every test passes there, and two real faults are fixed.** Nothing changes on Windows in this release.

## v1.516.0

**Never stalls, every cost counted, plain words.** The last of eight releases from the second review.

## v1.515.0

**Secrets at rest, and what leaves this PC.** The seventh of eight releases from the second review.

## v1.514.0

**What helpers, tools and servers report.** The sixth of eight releases from the second review.

## v1.513.0

**"Done" means done in a coding session.** The fifth of eight releases from the second review.

## v1.512.0

**Stop means stopped.** The fourth of eight releases from the second review.

## v1.511.0

**Every OK is for what will really happen.** The third of eight releases from the second review.

## v1.510.0

**Nothing is lost, part two.** The second of eight releases from the second review.

## v1.509.0

**Other computers and web pages stay outside.** A second review read the parts of Iron Jarvis the first one skipped (coding sessions, the browser, your other computers) and found 98 more things to fix.

## v1.508.0

**A coding session recovers from its own mistakes.** Read off a real coding session with a team, where helpers kept repeating failed steps.

## v1.507.1

**It learns from what went wrong.** The last part of the review fixes: Iron Jarvis now notices when something went wrong and asks whether to remember it.

## v1.506.0

**Ask for anything in chat.** The sixth part of the review fixes: things the app could do that chat could not reach, could not find, or could not point you to.

## v1.505.1

**It keeps going, and asks once.** The fifth part of the review fixes: places where work could hang with nobody told, and places where Iron Jarvis asked the same question again and again.

## v1.504.0

**Nothing is lost.** The fourth part of the review fixes: the places where your work, or the record of it, could disappear without a way back.
