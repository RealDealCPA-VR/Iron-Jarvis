# What changed in Iron Jarvis

*One paragraph per version, made from the Handbook's own sections. Settings › System › Updates shows the paragraph for the version you are on; the Handbook holds the full story of each change.*

## v1.600.0

**A rule works hands-off inside the limits you approved once.** A rule that starts agent work now runs on its own **inside limits you approve once**: rehearse it, read what it would do, and press **Keep it**.

## v1.599.0

**Iron Jarvis notices things about your work.** A few times a day Iron Jarvis looks at its own record of your work and may suggest something you probably want to pick up.

## v1.598.0

**Merge all that passed, in the morning.** The night shift leaves each task's work on its own branch.

## v1.597.0

**A second model can double-check finished work.** If you have more than one model account on the Several accounts card (Settings › Connections), Iron Jarvis asks you once, under a reply that made or changed a file:

## v1.596.0

**A skill suggestion says what was checked.** When you ask for the same kind of task on three different days within five weeks, in chat or as a task you start yourself, the night review writes one skill draft from that work and it waits in Improvements.

## v1.595.0

**"If" and "For each" in a workflow.** A workflow can now decide and repeat.

## v1.594.0

**It offers to run a task you repeat by itself.** When the three times you asked Iron Jarvis for the same kind of task in chat fell on a rhythm (the same day each week, say three Mondays), the night review adds a second suggestion beside the skill draft it wrote from those chats.

## v1.593.0

**Your own models set themselves up.** Each model on this PC or your network now says, on **Model servers**, what it is good at, in up to three plain lines:

## v1.592.1

**A weekly schedule runs on the day it names.** A schedule set for a day of the week now runs on that day.

## v1.592.0

**A skill draft from the work you keep asking for.** When you ask Iron Jarvis in chat for the same kind of task on three different days within five weeks (say, turning a bank export into a summary sheet), the night review notices it and writes a skill draft from those chats.

## v1.591.0

**Replies start sooner in a long chat.** In a long chat, Iron Jarvis now starts answering sooner.

## v1.590.0

**Every finished job says what was checked.** When a reply or a task makes or changes a file, Iron Jarvis now looks at the file itself before it calls the job done, and tells you what it found.

## v1.589.0

**Long files and long results are read to the end.** Some of what Iron Jarvis reads for you is long: a long Word, PDF or text document, a long list of files, a web page, a long answer from one of your extensions.

## v1.588.1

**Local only: memory search stays on this PC.** Two gaps in Local only are closed.

## v1.588.0

**Which model does best on your code, in the model menu.** The model menu of a coding session now says, under each model, what became of its work in the folder you are in.

## v1.587.0

**What you asked for that Iron Jarvis can't do yet.** When you ask for something Iron Jarvis has no feature for, it now keeps a note of it.

## v1.586.0

**Search by meaning, offered with what it costs here.** **Search by meaning** finds notes, files and memories by what they are about, not only by their words: "the angry email about shipping" finds the message that says "customer furious, parcel late".

## v1.585.0

**"When another task fails", and everything that runs by itself in one place.** Say it once in chat, such as "if the nightly report fails, tell me why and try once more", and Iron Jarvis makes **one rule** that starts when that task falls short.

## v1.584.0

**Phone chats, coding sessions and round-tables keep every message too.** Phone chats, coding sessions and round-tables no longer lose their oldest messages, and you can scroll back to them.

## v1.583.0

**See what is filling a chat.** When a chat gets long, a small meter under the message box shows how full it is.
