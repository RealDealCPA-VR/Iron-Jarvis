- **Defect:** SpawnAgentTool.execute mirrors DelegateTool ('spawn_agent mirrors the delegate tool', module docstring) but implements none of its safety machinery: DelegateTool refuses SUPERVISOR targets, refuses any target whose definition carries 'delegate' (the generalized anti-fork-bomb rule, delegate_tool.py:168-185), and enforces _MAX_DELEGATION_DEPTH=3 via _delegation_depth.
